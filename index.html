Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking, documents, and driving with EzSafar.

What documents are required to rent a self-drive car? expand_more

You simply need a valid Original Indian Driving License (minimum 1 year old) and an Aadhaar Card. Our team verifies them in under 2 minutes digitally via OTP or physical inspection at pickup.

How does the security deposit refund work? expand_more

For vehicles that require a refundable deposit (e.g. ₹2,000 to ₹5,000 for luxury SUVs), the full deposit is reversed to your original UPI or bank account within 2 hours of post-trip vehicle inspection.

Can I drive outside Haryana (Delhi, Punjab, HP, Rajasthan)? expand_more

Yes, 100%! All EzSafar vehicles carry all-India commercial permits. State entry taxes and standard toll charges are payable via the onboard Fastag, which you can easily recharge through any UPI app.

How do you verify pre-owned cars for sale? expand_more

Our certified automobile engineers check 140 technical parameters including chassis integrity, paint thickness, OBD diagnostics, suspension health, and past accident histories to guarantee uncompromised quality.
